# Download ConnectBot

Choose how you want to install ConnectBot on Android.

## Stable releases

- [Get it on Google Play](https://play.google.com/store/apps/details?id=org.connectbot).
- [Get it on F-Droid](https://f-droid.org/packages/org.connectbot/).
- [Download an APK from GitHub releases](https://github.com/connectbot/connectbot/releases).

Scan this code with your phone's camera to open the Google Play listing:

<QRCode value="https://play.google.com/store/apps/details?id=org.connectbot" />

Once installed, [connect to your first server](/guides/add-host/). You'll need a reachable server with SSH enabled and your own account credentials.

To check an installation from Google Play or GitHub Releases, follow [Verify your ConnectBot installation](/guides/verify-installation/).

## Early builds

ConnectBot is under active development. If you want to get an early build, you can join the open testing group (a.k.a., beta testers). You can also go to the next level and join the closed testing group (a.k.a., alpha testers).

### Beta Testers

To join the Beta Testing team, there is one step:

1. [Opt-in to ConnectBot testing](https://play.google.com/apps/testing/org.connectbot)

### Alpha Testers

To join the Alpha Testing Team, there are two steps:

1. [Opt-in to ConnectBot testing](https://play.google.com/apps/testing/org.connectbot)
2. [Join the ConnectBot Alpha group](https://groups.google.com/g/connectbot-alpha)
